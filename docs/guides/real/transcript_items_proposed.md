## 1. What Live Sale is for  
`Intro` · about 13 s

> Welcome to Live Sale. This is the main page of the shop. It is where every sale is recorded, one item at a time, at the moment it happens.

## 2. Why it is here  
`Intro` · about 24 s

> It is here because the shop's stock, cash and profit all come from what is recorded here. If a sale is not recorded, the stock figure is wrong, the cash does not add up, and nobody can tell what the shop really earned. Live Sale replaces writing sales down on paper at the end of the day.

## 3. What you use it for  
`Intro` · about 24 s

> You use it to sell items, to see what is in stock and at what price, to sell several items to one customer with M I, to register a customer while you sell, and to find and fix problems with the items, which the app marks as flags. Now let us see how, step by step.

## 4. Search the item, check quantity and price  
`How to` · about 21 s

> First, find the item. Type its name in Search, and the cards narrow down. You can also narrow the list by type and by group. Then check the quantity and the price on its card. Change them if the customer bought more than one, or agreed a different price.

*Changed: the old opening sentence "This is Live Sale." is removed (the intro now says it); type and group filters added.*

## 5. Who is it for? Then tap the red tick  
`How to` · about 14 s

> Say who it is for: a G M number, a phone number, a name, or a short label. Then tap the red tick, and the sale goes into the Sales Log.

## 6. WIC is a sale, GMC is shop use, several items go through MI  
`Law` · about 33 s

> When one customer buys several items, tap M I and add each to the basket. Company law: every walk-in sale goes in Live Sale with W I C on. G M C is only for the shop's own use, and it is not a sale. A customer buying several items is sold through M I: one basket, one receipt. Breaking it costs one penalty point. It exists so that every sale is recorded and every receipt is complete.

## 7. Wrong sale? Undo it in the Sales Log  
`Law` · about 29 s

> If you record a sale by mistake, undo it in the Sales Log. Law: receipts are issued from M I or the Sales Log, never typed into Customer Receipts. A sale the app refuses, such as a price below cost or not enough stock, is not forced: tell Joe. Breaking it costs one penalty point. A receipt typed by hand, or a forced sale, makes the books untrue.

## 8. Past days: + Past sale and the right date  
`Law` · about 18 s

> Law: past days are entered only with plus Past sale and the right sale date. The same work is never typed twice. Breaking it costs one penalty point. Wrong dates break the daily figures, and then the Sales Log cannot be trusted.

## 9. Item list: every good in the right group  
`Law` · about 18 s

> Law: once a week, item groups are checked: every good is in the right group. Breaking it costs one penalty point. Reports, filters and counts all depend on the group, and a wrongly grouped item hides in the wrong report.

## 10. Prices: everyone sells at the current price  
`Law` · about 16 s

> Law: once a month, the price list is checked: it is current, and every staff member has it. Breaking it costs one penalty point. A wrong price loses money on every sale of that item.

## 11. Know the words: SOH, SP, CP, WIC, GMC, CAB  
`Law` · about 26 s

> Law: app words. S O H is stock on hand. S P is selling price. C P is cost price. W I C is walk-in customer. G M C is Grony Multimedia as customer. C A B is cash at bank. Search the How To page before asking. Breaking it costs one penalty point. A misunderstood word becomes a wrong entry.

## 14. Law: Fix flags  
`Law` · about 30 s

> Now, the flags. This is company law: fix flags. Every flag on Live Sale is fixed as soon as it appears. It is never left for the next day, and never hidden by editing the item to make it disappear. Breaking it costs two penalty points. Open flags make prices wrong and stock untrue, and the real problem becomes harder to find. The Daily Checklist is where this law is enforced.
