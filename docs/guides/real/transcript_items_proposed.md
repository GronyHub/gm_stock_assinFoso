# Items / Live Sale video: the proposed transcript (version 2)

Updated for: laws limited to this page, violations read aloud, signature columns, flags last. Every chapter: title, kind, the exact words. About 2.5 spoken words a second.

**Not yet verified:** the per-flag steps come from `lib/flagGuide.ts`. The recording session checks each against the real app and skips and reports any flag with no working fix.


## 1. What Live Sale is for  
`Intro` · about 13 s

> Welcome to Live Sale. This is the main page of the shop. It is where every sale is recorded, one item at a time, at the moment it happens.

## 2. Why it is here  
`Intro` · about 24 s

> It is here because the shop's stock, cash and profit all come from what is recorded here. If a sale is not recorded, the stock figure is wrong, the cash does not add up, and nobody can tell what the shop really earned. Live Sale replaces writing sales down on paper at the end of the day.

## 3. What you use it for  
`Intro` · about 24 s

> You use it to sell items, to see what is in stock and at what price, to sell several items to one customer with M I, to register a customer while you sell, and to find and fix problems with the items, which the app marks as flags. Now let us see how, step by step.

## 4. Search the item, check quantity and price  
`How to` · about 21 s

> First, find the item. Type its name in Search, and the cards narrow down. You can also narrow the list by type and by group. Then check the quantity and the price on its card. Change them if the customer bought more than one, or agreed a different price.

## 5. Who is it for? Then tap the red tick  
`How to` · about 14 s

> Say who it is for: a G M number, a phone number, a name, or a short label. Then tap the red tick, and the sale goes into the Sales Log.

## 6. Several items, one customer: MI  
`How to` · about 11 s

> When one customer buys several items, tap M I, add each item to the basket, pick the customer, and issue one receipt for everything.

## 7. WIC is a sale, GMC is shop use  
`How to` · about 14 s

> Keep the switch on W I C for a sale to a customer. G M C is only for things the shop uses itself, and it is not a sale.

## 8. Wrong sale? Undo it in the Sales Log  
`How to` · about 21 s

> If you record a sale by mistake, undo it in the Sales Log. Do not tap again to correct it, because that records a second sale. If the app refuses a sale, such as a price below cost or not enough stock, do not force it. Tell Joe.

*The refused-sale rule is now a violation of the law below, not a law of its own.*

## 9. Law: Record every sale as soon as it happens  
`Law` · about 42 s

> Company law: record every sale as soon as it happens. A violation is: a sale not recorded in Live Sale as soon as it happens; shop use recorded as a sale, or a customer sale recorded as G M C; a customer not given a receipt; several items for one customer recorded as separate sales and not through M I; a new item created without first searching for an existing one; or a sale the app refused being forced through instead of telling Joe. Each costs one penalty point. It exists so that every sale is recorded and every receipt is complete.

## 10. Law: Keep every item in the right group  
`Law` · about 28 s

> Company law: keep every item in the right group. It is checked once a week. A violation is: the weekly group check not done on its day, or an item left in the wrong group, or in no group, after the check. Each costs one penalty point. Reports, filters and counts all depend on the group, and a wrongly grouped item hides in the wrong report.

## 11. Law: Keep the price list current  
`Law` · about 21 s

> Company law: keep the price list current. It is checked once a month. A violation is: the monthly price check not done, or an old price sold after the price list was updated. Each costs one penalty point. A wrong price loses money on every sale of that item.

## 12. Everything for this page: the Laws button  
`How to` · about 18 s

> Everything about this page, the laws, the violations, the penalty points, why each law exists, and how to do things, is on the Laws button, with this video and its written transcript. Tap any line to jump to that part.

## 13. Sign each law. Penalty points: the owner approves  
`Law` · about 32 s

> Every law has a code, and each staff member signs each law in their own column to show they have read it and will follow it. When a manager sees a violation, in the shop or on the camera, they log it, and you will be told. You can acknowledge it, or dispute it with a note. Twenty points is a three day suspension, thirty is one week, and fifty is dismissal. The owner approves every sanction.

## 14. Law: Fix flags as soon as they appear  
`Law` · about 32 s

> Now, flags. Company law: fix flags as soon as they appear. A violation is: a flag left open overnight, which costs two penalty points; an item edited only to make a flag disappear, which costs two; or a flag that cannot be fixed not being reported to Joe, which costs one. Open flags make prices wrong and stock untrue, and the real problem becomes harder to find. The Daily Checklist is where this law is enforced.

## 15. Where the flags are  
`Flags` · about 17 s

> A flag is the app telling you a record is wrong. Every flag on Live Sale is in the Flags dropdown on the toolbar, with the number open next to each. Here is how each one is fixed.

## 16. Flag: No Selling Price  
`Flag` · about 14 s

> The item has no selling price, or the price is zero. Tap the item to open its sheet. Tap Edit. Type the selling price. Tap Save. The flag clears at once.

## 17. Flag: No Cost Price (goods)  
`Flag` · about 16 s

> A goods item has no cost price, or it is zero. Open the item and tap Edit. Type the cost price from the latest bill. Tap Save. No bill to read it from? Tell Joe.

## 18. Flag: No Group  
`Flag` · about 12 s

> The item is not in any group. Open the item and tap Edit. Pick an existing group, or type a new one if none fits. Tap Save.

## 19. Flag: Empty Row  
`Flag` · about 14 s

> An item row has no name or details, so it cannot be sold. Open the row. Fill in the item name, group and prices, or tell Joe if it should be deleted.

## 20. Flag: Duplicate Items  
`Flag` · about 20 s

> Two items look like the same product under slightly different names. Open the flag to see each pair side by side. Tap Keep on the name you want to keep. The history merges into it. If they are two different products, tap Different, Not a Duplicate.

## 21. Flag: Unlinked Sales  
`Flag` · about 14 s

> Past sale lines match an item by name but were never linked to it. Open the flag. Each row says how many lines match. Tap Link N sales to this item.

## 22. Flag: Service Violations  
`Flag` · about 23 s

> A service item carries stock, a bill, a count or GMC use. Services have no stock. Open the flag to see the affected services. Find the wrongly logged bill, count or GMC entry and correct or remove it. Clear any cost price on the service itself. Cost belongs on the material it uses.

## 23. Flag: Unknown Quantity  
`Flag` · about 10 s

> An old sale line has no quantity. Open the flag and each item. Set the quantity from the receipt or ask Joe.

## 24. Flag: GMC Pack Overage  
`Flag` · about 18 s

> The shop has used more of a pack than it was given. Open the item and check the latest pack tap. Tap in the missing pack, or correct the overused GMC entries. Tell Joe if it still does not match.

## 25. Flag: Net Gain  
`Flag` · about 16 s

> Over all its counts, gains outweigh losses. Gains should never beat losses. Open the item and its count history. Look for a miscount, an unrecorded receipt or a duplicate. Correct the record that caused it.

## 26. Flag: Needs Review  
`Flag` · about 10 s

> An old stub item could not be identified. Open the flag. Match it to the right item, or merge it.

## 27. Flag: Alias Needs Check  
`Flag` · about 13 s

> An old name was matched to an item by a best guess. Open the flag. If the match is right, confirm it. If not, use Merge to fix it.

## 28. Flag: Ledger Notes  
`Flag` · about 10 s

> Old ledger cells held text instead of a quantity. Open the flag and read each note. Confirm it is not a sale.

## 29. Flag: Negative Stock  
`Flag` · about 16 s

> Stock on hand has gone below zero. Count the item and enter what is on the shelf. If it is not due for a count, ask Joe to check for a purchase bill that was never entered.
