# Brief: managers-only video (Staff management > managers-only section, and the Flags laws)

For the voice session. One video, 4 to 5 minutes, British voice as before. Audience: Joe (manager at the shop) and Grony (owner, in the UK). Real screenshots of the live app, no staged data.

## What it must cover, in this order (each part ends with the law code and its points)
1. **Why managers have their own laws.** Joe is at the shop with the staff; Grony is not. Joe checks and decides for the staff; Grony checks and decides for Joe. Nobody decides their own case. (Laws about the laws: points are automatic.)
2. **The CCTV review.** Days when staff were at work and Joe had not clocked in. The pop-up, the windows to watch, the sales entered in those times. Confirm sales and laws, then log a violation or send a question. Law: *Complete each CCTV review within 48 hours* (1 point). Show **Not now: remind me in...** (30 minutes to 12 hours).
3. **Decisions waiting.** Clock-out times to approve (the app clocks forgetful staff out at their last shop action; they tap OK or send a correct time), sanctions to approve, violations to confirm or review, disputes. Law: *Decide waiting approvals within 48 hours* (1 point). Show Grony's **Prompt Joe to check** button.
4. **Logging a violation.** Penalties: pick the law, pick the violation line, write what you saw. Staff acknowledge or dispute. Points from the app (missed checklist tasks, unsigned laws, quizzes and videos not done) need no logging.
5. **Flags.** Every flag in the app is fixed by managers only. Show the Flags hub, one flag from each page, and the single "Fix flags" law.
6. **The closing report.** Daily Checklist, top card, from 5pm (can be answered early). It feeds the missing-closing-report flag and the dress-code flag.
7. **Voice.** The red microphone: say where to go ("open customers"), search ("search ghana card"), or a sale in this order: item, quantity, price, who.
